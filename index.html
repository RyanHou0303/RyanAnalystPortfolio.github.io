Asian Option Pricing
Derivatives pricing · Residual correction · Stochastic volatility
Python library for pricing arithmetic Asian options by combining Monte Carlo benchmarks, classical analytical approximations, and a machine learning residual-correction layer that learns the systematic bias of fast pricing formulas.
  • Corrected Turnbull-Wakeman approximation bias by learning residuals against a high-precision control-variate Monte Carlo benchmark.
  • Preserved the speed and interpretability of analytical pricing while improving accuracy across moneyness, volatility, maturity, monitoring frequency, and spot levels.
  • Extended testing to Heston and SABR stochastic-volatility settings, Greek accuracy comparisons, and flexible averaging windows [T1, T2].
